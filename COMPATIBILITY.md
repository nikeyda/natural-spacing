# Compatibility Status

No production platform adapter has been released.

Experimental status is intentional at this milestone. Passing the language-neutral fixtures is necessary but does not substitute for real platform and IME acceptance.

See [the Unicode segmentation matrix](docs/unicode-segmentation-matrix.md) for per-core version gaps and [the platform acceptance protocol](docs/platform-acceptance.md) for required real-input evidence.
